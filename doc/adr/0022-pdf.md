# 0022. PDF in notes

Status: accepted, 2026-11-13

## Context

Notes embed PDFs as Obsidian does: `![[doc.pdf]]`, `![[doc.pdf#page=3]]`, and link them with
`[[doc.pdf]]`. The live preview showed them as links; the index kept PDFs with their metadata
only. The owner answered a question round (2026-11-13).

## Decision

- An embedded PDF alone on its line shows a preview of its page (`#page=N`, the first
  otherwise), sized and resized as images are (ADR 0020), with "сторінка 3 з 12" under it.
- A click on the preview, or on a link to a PDF, opens the PDF in Knotes in place of the note:
  a viewer (PDFKit) in the note column, scrolling and zooming, at the page linked; the
  toolbar's Back returns to the note (owner's choice over Quick Look).
- A PDF dropped from Finder is copied to `.knotes/attachments` and embedded, as images are.
- The index takes in PDFs' text; the search in the note list finds PDFs by it, each as an
  entry of its own that opens the viewer.

## Consequences

- With a click opening the viewer, the embed's markup is reached with the keyboard (or a
  click beside the preview).
