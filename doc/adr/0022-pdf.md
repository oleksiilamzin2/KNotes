# 0022. PDF in notes

Status: accepted, 2026-11-13

## Context

Notes embed PDFs as Obsidian does: `![[doc.pdf]]`, `![[doc.pdf#page=3]]`, and link them with
`[[doc.pdf]]`. The live preview showed them as links; the index kept PDFs with their metadata
only. The owner answered a question round (2026-11-13).

## Decision

- An embedded PDF alone on its line shows a preview of its page (`#page=N`, the first
  otherwise), sized and resized as images are (ADR 0020), with "сторінка 3 з 12" under it.
- A click on the preview, or on a link to a PDF, opens the PDF in Knotes in place of the note:
  a viewer (PDFKit) in the note column, scrolling and zooming, at the page linked; the
  toolbar's Back returns to the note (owner's choice over Quick Look).
- A PDF dropped from Finder is copied to `.knotes/attachments` and embedded, as images are.
- The index takes in PDFs' text; the search in the note list finds PDFs by it, each as an
  entry of its own that opens the viewer.

## Consequences

- With a click opening the viewer, the embed's markup is reached with the keyboard (or a
  click beside the preview).

## Built (2026-11-13)

- A PDF embed is an `ImageRun` marked as a PDF, with its page from `#page=N`. Its preview is
  the page drawn by CoreGraphics (`CGPDFPage`, its crop box and rotation) on white, at the
  size it shows at, in the background as images are decoded (ADR 0020); a thin edge marks the
  page; "сторінка 2 з 3" is drawn under it in room the line keeps.
- A click on the preview goes through the same path as a click on a link to the PDF: the
  window shows the PDF in the note column, the note's view hidden, the toolbar naming the
  file; the PDF is a history entry, so Back returns to the note.
- The viewer is Knotes's own (owner's request: keep only the page shown and two above and two
  below it): pages one under another fitted to the column, zoomed by pinching, each drawn by
  CoreGraphics in the background (one at a time; a page let go before its turn is skipped)
  and let go once it is more than two pages from the one shown and out of view; the viewer
  goes with all it drew when the PDF is left. PDFKit's `PDFView` kept every page it drew
  while it lived, and after leaving a PDF scrolled through some 55 MB stayed. A canvas the
  size of the visible part draws what shows: drawn into the document view itself, the pages'
  whole height was layer tiles let go of only in batches, and memory piled up as pages went
  by. With 1000 A4 pages (M5 Pro, release): 46 MB before, about 190 MB while scrolling
  (steady), 58 MB two seconds after leaving.
- A new width or zoom draws the pages again; until each is, it shows as it was, stretched (the
  blank page between flickered, owner's report). A new width draws them again only once it has
  not changed for 200 ms: dragging a divider is no live resize for the views beside it, and
  drawing every step made the pages blur and sharpen over and over (owner's report). The place
  at the top (a page and how far down it) stays at the top as the width changes: the pages
  grow or shrink with the width, and the same scroll offset showed another page. A preview
  widened in a note shows its smaller picture stretched too (images as well, ADR 0020).
- Text is selected with the mouse (a drag, a double click for a word, a triple click for a
  line, Shift-click to extend; across pages too) and copied with Edit > Copy or the context
  menu (owner's request, after the first own viewer had dropped PDFKit's selection). A page's
  text and its characters' boxes are read through PDFKit when the page is clicked or drawn
  with something on it selected, into Knotes's own data, the PDFKit document let go at once;
  the text of pages neither in view nor selected is let go (owner's choice). Each character's
  box is a one-character `PDFSelection`'s: `characterBoundsAtIndex` counts without the line
  breaks PDFKit puts in the page's text, so its boxes drift a character per line. A page of
  2400 characters (M5 Pro, release): about 4 ms, half of it the boxes; the first page read
  in a run also loads PDFKit (about 50 ms) unless the index has. Where the caret goes, words
  and lines, and the highlight (multiplied over the page, as tall as the line) are Knotes's
  (`knotes-pdf`'s `PageText`).
- Cmd+F finds in the PDF shown with the note's find bar, which the viewer sits under: a
  thread reads the pages' text one after another through PDFKit (each let go after its page)
  and keeps only the places found, as character ranges; the count reads "Пошук…" meanwhile,
  and a newer search or a closed bar calls it off. Places on pages in view are highlighted,
  the current one in the find colour, the others pale yellow (their pages' text read as for a
  selection); stepping brings the next a third of the way down the view.
- A document opened from a content search, a PDF or a note, shows Cmd+F's bar with the
  search's first word (owner's request): its places found and highlighted, the first from the
  page shown on (a note's from its start) in view, stepped through with the bar's arrows or
  Cmd+G. The focus stays where it was, so arrows go on through the results in the list. A link
  naming a PDF's page keeps its page.
- Keys move through the pages as in Preview (owner's polish list): space and Page Down a screen
  on, less 40 points kept in view (Shift+space and Page Up back), the arrows up and down 40
  points, left and right a page, Cmd+up and down, Home and End the first and last page. View
  has Zoom In, Zoom Out and Actual Size (Cmd+=, Cmd+-, Cmd+0), steps of 1.25 between 0.5 and
  4, sent up the responder chain to the viewer. A PDF opened from the note (a link or its
  preview clicked) takes the keys; one opened from the list leaves the focus there, as a note
  does.
- As the pages move (scrolled, turned, jumped to, and when the PDF opens) a small plate at
  the bottom of the viewer says "сторінка 3 з 12" and fades out 1.5 s after the last move, as
  Preview's does (owner's request). The scroll view lays out its views by frame and counts
  from its top: the plate is placed by frame from its bottom.
- The index reads PDFs' text through PDFKit (`knotes-pdf`, behind a `DocumentText` trait the
  indexer takes) into plain blocks of paragraphs, no Markdown read into them (a `#word` in a
  PDF is no tag); the schema's version went to 13 so indexed vaults read their PDFs. Note
  rows include PDFs; the folder lists leave them out, the search's results show them, and a
  search in a folder covers its PDFs.
