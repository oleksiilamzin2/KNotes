# 0024. Tabs in the sidebar

Status: accepted, 2026-11-13

## Context

Knotes shows one document at a time, with a back/forward history. The owner asked for tabs: a
third segment beside "Теки | Теги" over the sidebar, listing the documents open, and answered
a question round (2026-11-13).

## Decision

- The sidebar's switch has a segment "Вкладки", first ("Вкладки | Теки | Теги", owner's
  order): the sidebar lists the tabs, one row per
  document (a note or a PDF), the one shown highlighted. A click on a row shows its document;
  a cross on the row closes it. The note list keeps what it showed.
- A note opens in the tab shown (the first opened makes a tab); "Відкрити в новій вкладці" in
  the list's menu, or in a link's context menu in a note, opens it in a new one. A PDF opens in a new tab right after the one shown,
  and that tab shows (owner's choice, after a first version that made a tab of every
  document). A document that has a tab brings it back instead. Closing the tab shown shows the
  next one (the one before it when it was last); closing the last tab leaves the note column
  empty.
- A document is shown again where it was: for a note, the paragraph at the top and the
  selection; for a PDF, the place at the top. Places are kept per document, so a tab that
  moved on to another note loses none.
- The back/forward history stays one for the window, as before (owner's choice), not one per
  tab.
- Tabs and their places are kept between launches, per vault, in Knotes's data for the vault
  (beside its index, not in the vault: nothing to sync or commit).
- A document renamed or moved keeps its tab; a deleted one loses it.
- Cmd+W closes the tab shown (the window when there is none); Shift+Cmd+W closes the window
  (owner's request, after a first version that left Cmd+W to the window). A tab's context
  menu closes it or every other tab.

## Consequences

- Places of documents no tab holds last only for the session.

## Built (2026-11-13)

- The sidebar switch's third segment (`square.on.square`) lists the tabs in the sidebar's
  outline view, a document icon and the name as the toolbar shows it (no `.md` or `.pdf`), a
  cross at the row's end that shows only with the pointer over the row, as Safari's (each row
  tracks the pointer; one that moves under a still pointer as tabs close checks it); the tab shown is the selected row. Every launch starts on the
  folders (owner's choice): the switch lasts the session, no longer kept in `settings-v2.json`.
- Every way of opening a document goes through the window's opening of a note or a PDF, so
  the list, links, search results, the history and new notes all follow the same rule.
- A tab's place is taken as its document is left: a note's paragraph at the top (its UTF-16
  offset, as the outline finds it) and its selection; a PDF's page shown and how far down it
  the top of the view is. Coming back scrolls there before a search's words are shown (they
  win), and a link naming a PDF page wins over the PDF's place. A tab closed gives its place
  to no other: only the document of the tab shown is measured.
- `tabs.json` beside the vault's index holds the tabs, their documents' places and the tab
  shown; it is
  written as documents are left (on every switch, on closing a window, on quitting) and when
  a tab closes, and read on the vault's first snapshot, the tab shown then opened again.
  Tabs of documents gone from the vault are dropped then and on every snapshot; moves rename
  them as they rename the history.
- With no document open (at launch before any, or once the last tab closes) the note column
  says "Немає відкритого документа" and hides the text view, which no longer takes typing:
  text typed there went nowhere.
